Who We Are

We are a team of three students studying Web Development. We created Book Order Tracker as a project for our web course. Our website helps users order educational and programming books in a simple and easy way.

Our Website

Book Order Tracker is an online platform where users can choose programming books such as Java, Python, HTML and CSS, and Data Structures. Users can select the quantity, delivery option, and payment method to place their orders.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make ordering educational books easier, faster, and more convenient for everyone.

Our Objectives

Our objective is to provide an easy way for users to find and order programming books and improve their knowledge and technical skills.

Why Choose Us?

We offer a simple ordering process that allows users to select books, quantities, delivery options, and payment methods easily.
